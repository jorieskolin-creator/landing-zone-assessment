Architect’s verdict

The FinOps Engine is already a Landing Zone Engine with the wrong knowledge pack.

The cognitive model in the thinking-flow visual is the product. Dual-stream forensic audit, independent verification, deterministic pair scoring, tactic permissioning, and a code-owned Quality Gate transfer without change of meaning. What does not transfer is the subject: cloud-financial practice versus cloud-foundation control plane.

Keep

Execution kernel, acquisition spine, pair math, QG, tactic ID contract, RunTrace.

Replace

A–F FinOps rubric, personas, prompts, keywords, tactics, A1 tagging analyzer, report copy.

Extend

Step 0 scope, Class 1 inventory lane, Class 3 workshop objects, provider packs, expert lock.

Reuse the kernel. Replace the pack.

Packets, pairs, gates, unknown-is-not-zero, tactic IDs, fact-check and publication control are the product. Categories, criteria, questions, personas and tactics are data.

Control plane first.

Landing-zone truth lives in hierarchy, identity bindings, policy attachments, shared networks and logging destinations. Documents explain intent. Workshops explain ownership. Their mechanics may align; their authority remains separate.

Unknown is not zero. N/A is not Crawl.

Silence after only a workshop remains unknown. A silent design area does not receive a red score and does not generate remediation tactics. Out-of-scope clouds are not scored.

Establish the truth first. Design the implementation second.

Accelerators, Service Portfolio items and technical options must not influence what the Engine concludes about the customer’s current platform.

The Engine narrows. Experts judge. The customer decides.

GO / WARN / BLOCK are evidence sufficiency, not customer pass or fail. The Summary is Master Data for discussion, not a mandatory verdict.

Recommended shape

One kernel. Two domain packs. FinOps remains A–F. Landing Zone is A–H. They share packets, pairs, gates and dispatch. They never share criteria, quotes or tactics.

Do not fork the repository into a copy. Introduce a domain-pack interface at src/knowledge_base/, widen DomainId, and load LZ JSON. The FinOps A1 tagging analyzer stays FinOps-only.

Landing-zone truth model

Platform configuration, documents and workshops are not interchangeable.

This is the main cognitive addition FinOps did not need. FinOps can live on documents, tables and interviews. Landing Zone truth lives first in the control plane. The rest of the thinking flow is downstream of that rank.

01

Platform

Is it so now?

Live or exported inventory: hierarchy, policy and SCP assignments, identity bindings, hub networks, log archives, vending. Resolves existence, attachment and enforcement.

02

Documents & IaC

Intended state

Design, naming, RACI, Bicep / Terraform / accelerator repos. Drift between repo and tenant is itself a finding — not a wash.

03

Workshops

Operating model

How a new environment is requested, who can create subscriptions, why an exception exists, whether break-glass is rehearsed. Raises or lowers confidence. Cannot award Embedded for a missing preventive guardrail.

Unknown is not zero

Silence after a scoped collector can become tested absence. Silence after only a workshop remains unknown. A silent design area does not receive a red score and does not generate remediation tactics.

Contradiction, not alignment

A design document that says Deny, while the assignment is Audit, is a contradiction. Workshops explain it. They do not vote it away.

GO / WARN / BLOCK

Evidence sufficiency — not customer pass or fail. BLOCK starts a decision conversation. It does not end the assessment.

Build sequence

Freeze the pack. Parameterize IDs. Then add the inventory lane.

Do not start with accelerators, a new UI, or the Implementation Model. Those are downstream of a loadable domain pack and honest scoring. P7 is a second pipeline, after locked findings.
