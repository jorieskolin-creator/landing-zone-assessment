Interview & Evidence Discovery Questionnaire

A structured workshop aid for capturing customer knowledge before evidence is analyzed by the Landing Zone Engine. The questions support dialogue, reveal tacit practices, and identify inventory, documents and owners worth collecting. Interview answers are discovery inputs — not landing-zone scores. The control plane remains the system of record.

How to use this questionnaire Ask the open question, use the hints only when useful, capture what the participant actually describes, and request a recent example. When an artifact, export or live inventory can corroborate the statement, record it under Evidence / artifact lead.
Evidence path
Interview observation → Evidence lead → Evidence acquisition → Landing Zone Engine verification
Workshop does not replace inventory A confident answer without a control-plane lead is still unverified. Prefer hierarchy exports, role assignments, policy, logs and IaC over recollection.
