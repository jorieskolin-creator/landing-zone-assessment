Bounded register of publisher-verified sources for the Landing Zone Engine. It owns identity, authority tier, location, licensing and coverage. It does not score a landing zone, prove compliance, or replace control-plane evidence.

Status: APPROVED · Evidence and source date 2026-09-15 · 43 sources · 8 domains · 80 criteria. This register is finalized. It is not a draft.

00Authority boundary

Registration is identity. Scoring is inventory.

This register owns stable source identity, exact publication identity, authority tier and type, official location, edition or date, verification metadata, licensing boundary, bounded A–H coverage and bounded criterion coverage hints (A1–H5 and AP-A1–AP-H5). It does not contain exact clause locators or case evidence. Criterion objects own exact locators, supported claims, mapping rationale, applicability and exclusions. Registration alone cannot satisfy a control, prove a landing zone, or authorize a governance decision.

01

TIER 1 — Normative

Binding legislation. A source is normative only when its jurisdictional, material, actor, role and temporal applicability conditions are established for the assessed estate. Landing-zone assessments rarely score TIER_1 directly; NIS2/GDPR support logging, incident, residency and governance interpretation.

02

TIER 2 — Authoritative

Official provider reference architectures (CAF, Control Tower, GCP landing-zone series), product documentation used as locator identity, government and intergovernmental guidance, and published consensus standards. These sources support interpretation and evidence contracts but must not be represented as legislation or as proof that a customer implemented them.

03

TIER 3 — Supporting

Recognized community benchmarks (CIS, CSA CCM) and implementation accelerators. They may support tests and examples but cannot independently establish landing-zone maturity, legal obligations or a GO on a criterion.

01Admission

What a source must be before it may support a finding.

Mapping requirements (criterion objects)

  1. Use the exact registered source ID, source version or date, verification status and verification date from the sealed baseline context.
  2. Identify a precise section, design area, control family, principle or product object; generic thematic locators are prohibited in criterion objects.
  3. State the criterion-specific supported claim, relationship, mapping rationale, applicability conditions and exclusions.
  4. Do not infer landing-zone maturity, control satisfaction, legal applicability or decision authority from source registration or domain coverage.
  5. Do not combine multiple instruments, publications or mutable collections behind one source ID.
  6. Provider packs (Azure, AWS, GCP) are selected in Step 0. Out-of-scope cloud sources are not a silent zero.
  7. Workshop testimony cannot replace a control-plane object for a GO on that criterion.

Decision-eligible requirements

  1. The register and approval record are APPROVED and the source record is VERIFIED.
  2. The record identifies one stable instrument or publication with an official publisher location, exact edition or date, authority tier, authority type and applicability conditions.
  3. The source is in force or published and is not a draft, proposal, collection page used as the scored edition, mutable topic page, withdrawn, superseded or under development.
  4. VERIFIED means source governance checked the publisher, title, instrument identifier, version or date, publication status, canonical location, authority classification and licensing boundary — not only URL reachability.
  5. Licensed standards (ISO, CIS numbered editions, CSA CCM) remain metadata- and locator-only unless separate, explicit usage rights permit storage or transmission of protected content.
  6. Implementation accelerators (GitHub ALZ, AFT/LZA, terraform-example-foundation) cannot independently establish a capability score.
  7. A VERIFIED record may be used only within its stated authority boundary and never substitutes for assessment-time applicability or live inventory.
